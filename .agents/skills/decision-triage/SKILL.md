---
name: decision-triage
description: Classify loop uncertainty into automatic work, deferred review or mandatory preapproval without resolving human decisions.
---
Use the current task and approved policy. Production/publication, destructive operations, expanded confidential access, material authentication changes, spending, protection changes and policy relaxation require prior human approval. Medium internal design/dependencies may proceed only within approved local scope and remain unmergeable until reviewed. Record the question, options and affected tasks. Never fabricate a human actor or approval, reuse a stale hash, or treat inactivity as consent. Keep independent tasks runnable when their scope is unaffected.
