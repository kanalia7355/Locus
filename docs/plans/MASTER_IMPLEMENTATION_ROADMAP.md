## 1. Roadmap rules

1. **The original approved design is the completion authority.** Existing product phases `P0–P5` (`PH-ROADMAP-0`–`PH-ROADMAP-5`), product MVP slices `MVP-1`–`MVP-6`, autonomous-loop phases 1–6 (`LOOP-PHASE-1`–`LOOP-PHASE-6`), and hybrid execution steps `H1–H6` remain distinct numbered families. These namespaced aliases add no new source phases. A phase number in one family does not complete a different family. Product/development completion is the conjunction of all applicable requirements in the stable catalog and the accepted task/phase gates.
2. **Prototype scope is an implementation subset, not final closure.** Prototype-approved omissions (for example no real data, no production TLS, MFA prototype omission, production DB, audit retention, runner isolation) remain in the final backlog unless the owner explicitly rules a requirement out of final scope. “Deferred” is not “out of scope.”
3. **Evidence is conjunctive.** For each work package: source-grounded acceptance, implementation at exact SHA, mandatory local QA, independent review when required, exact-head required CI, correct integration, and owner decisions for any security/scope-dependent choice. Code/tests existing alone is insufficient. Unavailable/skipped checks are blocked/skipped, never passed. Mocks and synthetic tests prove only their stated boundaries.
4. **Approval is not activation.** ADR/plan approval does not authorize credentials, real data, external egress, live runner execution, service operation or main mutation unless the exact separate activation permission and technical gates are documented.
5. **Dependencies and parallelism.** Parallelize only independent, disjoint work with explicit base syncing and conflict ownership. Material security/data-model changes need one fresh read-only review; no routine implementation agents. Pipeline jobs currently assigned by the owner/orchestrator are exceptional task assignments, not general permission to spawn agents.
6. **Bounded repair.** Each task has maximum three implementation/verification attempts including CI repair and no more than two review fix/re-review cycles. Preserve prior attempts and unresolved operations. Stop on uncertainty; do not retry external side effects blindly.
7. **Delivery policy and technical gates remain distinct.** The owner formally adopted post-phase automatic main integration on 2026-11-09 (OWNER_DECISION_REGISTER.md §B.2): implementation/review/QA, completion conditions, final audit, and all-CI-green precede integration and version bump; use a PR and merge after conditions if required. This does not approve PR-free main mutation, bypass branch protection, or establish incomplete broker/base-binding guarantees. Follow the active task authorization for branch CI; do not push directly to main or weaken protection. Before declaring product/system final complete, verify that the actual integration mechanism satisfies the adopted protected-branch and technical requirements.
